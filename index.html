COMPANY PROFILE · ABOUT US

Who Is Nal Al Tsheed?

Introduction

The unique geographic location of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia made it an important centre for global trade and investment, helping to attract investors and increasing economic activity in the country in various fields.

The contracting sector is one of many countries’ most important economic bases. Saudi Arabia’s construction sector drives the country’s growth, moving economic activity in many industries and associated activities. Also, a report issued by the Council of Saudi Chambers indicates that the construction sector’s recovery ensures the growth of activity in the development sectors and industries. It means that the construction sector is the engine of growth in the national economy.

Who Is Nal Al Tsheed?

Nal Al Tsheed is an internationally owned Saudi Arabian company that offers Engineering, Construction, and Construction Project Management.

Nal Al Tsheed is dedicated to organisations and individuals seeking construction services.

COMPANY PROFILE · APPROACH

Expertise & Client Approach

Who We Serve

To power distribution and transmission companies, parastatals, oil, water, and pipeline companies, public works agencies, municipalities, and commercial and residential real estate developers.

Areas of Expertise

Our area of expertise includes, but is not limited to, building technology, Architectural and civil work, and structural engineering.

How We Work With Clients

We work closely with our clients to accurately interpret their dreams/visions in drawings and bring them to the desired reality through construction solutions that meet their needs.

We communicate with our clients throughout the construction process to inform them of progress and ensure that our project stays on schedule and within budget.
